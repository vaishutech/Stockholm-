Welcome to Venice of Scandinavia

Stockholm, the capital of Sweden, is a stunning city built on 14 islands connected by over 50 bridges, earning it the nickname "Venice of the North." It blends historic charm with modern design, featuring the medieval old town, Gamla Stan, the Royal Palace, and unique museums like the Vasa Museum and ABBA Museum. Surrounded by water and greenery, Stockholm is eco-friendly, clean, and known for its vibrant culture, tech innovation, and love for "fika," a relaxing coffee break. It’s a city where history, nature, and modern life come together beautifully.
